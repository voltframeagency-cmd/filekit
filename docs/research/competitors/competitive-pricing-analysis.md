**The market’s real pricing moat is transparent monetization, not cheaper subscriptions.**

## 📋 **FileKit Proposed Pricing Architecture**

| Tier | Price | Core entitlement |
|---|---:|---|
| **Free Local** | €0 | Unlimited supported browser-side tasks, no account, no card |
| **Job Pass** | €4.90 one-time | 7 days of premium processing; **never renews** |
| **Pro** | €9 monthly / €59 annually | Batch, OCR, larger files, server allowance, presets & history |
| **Team** | €12 monthly / €8 annually per seat | Shared workspace, controls, consolidated billing & team credits (3-seat min) |
| **API** | Usage-based | Prepaid non-expiring credits; **charge successful jobs only** |

### Commercial Safety Rules:
1. No card required for free local processing.
2. No 28-day consumer billing.
3. No silent renewal on Job Pass.
4. No annual plan preselection.
5. Display renewal amount beside every purchase CTA.
