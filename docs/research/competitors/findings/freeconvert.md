# FreeConvert Competitor Audit Findings

## 1. Overview
- **Slug**: `freeconvert`
- **URL**: `https://www.freeconvert.com`
- **Category**: Broad Format Converter
- **Audited Tools**: Image Converter, PDF Compressor, Video Converter, Audio Converter.

## 2. Interaction & Workflow Analysis
- **Clicks to Upload**: 1 click (`Choose Files`).
- **Clicks to Process**: 2 clicks (Select format + click `Convert`).
- **Clicks to Download**: 1 click (`Download`).
- **Live Preview**: Progressive disclosure panel for bitrate, resolution, and DPI settings.

## 3. Visual System & Components
- **Color Palette**: Slate Blue (`#2B6CB0`), White background, Heavy ad containers.
- **Ad Density**: High visual clutter caused by vertical skyscraper ad banners flanking the central dropzone.

## 4. Monetization & Trust Communication
- **Monetization**: Display ads + file size limits (1GB free limit).
- **Trust Messaging**: Emphasizes 256-bit SSL encryption and auto-deletion after 2 hours.

## 5. FileKit Recommendations
- **AVOID**: Heavy ad banner layouts and deceptive download button placement.
- **ADAPT**: Progressive disclosure for advanced output settings.
