- **Slug**: `tinywow`

## 2. Interaction & Workflow Analysis

- **Clicks to Process**: 1 click.

- **Color Palette**: Multi-color portals (Purple, Orange, Crimson, Blue, Teal).

## 4. Monetization & Trust Communication
- **Monetization**: Free with display ad monetization; $5.99/mo Pro option for ad-free usage.
- **Friction**: Bot verification / CAPTCHA gates interrupt free tool execution.

## 5. FileKit Recommendations
- **AVOID**: CAPTCHA gates and bot checks before downloading.
- **ADAPT**: Search bar hero for rapid tool discovery.
