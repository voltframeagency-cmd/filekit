## 📋 **The Founder Playbook**

### Modelled Revenue Quality Tiers

* **Tier A (Durable)**: Adobe, CloudConvert ($8M–$20M), iLovePDF ($10M–$25M), Sejda ($5M–$12M).
* **Tier B (Scalable / Exposed)**: Smallpdf ($8M–$18M), FreeConvert ($6M–$18M), Convertio ($5M–$15M).
* **Tier C (Subsidized / Free)**: PDF24 ($2M–$7M), Squoosh ($0 direct), TinyWow ($0.5M–$3M).
* **Tier D (High Collection / Low Trust)**: PDFAid ($10M–$35M gross collections), ZenDocs ($8M–$25M gross collections).

### Growth Strategy for FileKit:
1. Build exact-intent search engine pages (`/compress-pdf-to-1mb`, `/compress-image-to-200kb`) powered by reusable local engines.
2. Build 2 revenue layers: Consumer Job Pass & Pro + B2B Team & API credits.
3. Keep paid search acquisition under 10% until contribution margin > 80% and LTV/CAC > 3.
